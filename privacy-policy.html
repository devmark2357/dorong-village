3. 다른 이용자에게 보이는 정보

닉네임 · 마을 이름 · 마을 모습 · 좋아요 수 · 방명록 도장은 다른 이용자의 친구 목록 · 순위 · 망원경 화면에 보입니다. 방명록에 글을 쓰는 기능은 서로 친구인 사이에서만 열리며, 마을 주인은 친구를 언제든 끊을 수 있고 부적절한 글을 신고 · 차단할 수 있습니다.

4. 이용자의 권리와 계정 삭제

· 게임 안 설정 › 계정 › 내 마을 지우기에서 언제든 계정을 지울 수 있습니다. 마을 · 진행 · 재화 · 친구 · 방명록 · 옷장이 지워지며 되돌릴 수 없습니다. 결제 기록만 법정 기간 동안 보관합니다.
· 직접 삭제가 어려우면 아래 메일로 요청하면 확인 후 지웁니다.
· 자기 정보의 열람 · 정정 · 처리정지를 요청할 수 있습니다.
· Android: 설정 › 개인정보 보호 › 광고에서 광고 ID를 재설정 · 삭제할 수 있고, iOS: 설정 › 개인정보 보호 및 보안 › 추적에서 앱별 추적을 끌 수 있습니다.

5. 아동

게임은 만 14세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않으며, 아동의 개인정보를 알면서 수집하지 않습니다.

6. 안전성 확보

모든 전송은 HTTPS로 암호화되고, 서버 접근은 관리자 계정으로만 제한합니다. 기기 인증값은 원문 대신 해시로 저장합니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Service: Dorong Dorong Slow Village (Android · iOS · web) · Operator: LABOON Inc.

LABOON Inc. ("we") operates the mobile game Dorong Dorong Slow Village (the "Game"). The Game does not ask for your name, phone number or any government ID. You can start as a guest; you may link a Google or Apple account to continue on another device.

1. Information we process

· Guest account ID (random), nickname, village name and code, access logs (last seen, app version, platform, device model) — account operation, friends and rankings, abuse prevention — until account deletion.
· If you link Google/Apple: the account identifier and verified email they provide — account linking and recovery — until deletion or unlinking.
· Cloud save (residents, buildings, decorations, currency, settings) — until account deletion.
· Friend features (follows, likes, guestbook stamps and messages, a summary of your village's look) — until account deletion (stamps: 14 days).
· Outfit orders (outfit description, an optional outfit photo, the resulting drawing) — the photo is deleted right after the drawing is made; drawings are kept until account deletion.
· Purchase records (store order ID, product, amount, time — no payment card data) — 5 years (Korean e-commerce law).
· Support messages, app version, device model — 3 years after resolution.
· App error logs (no name, email or IP address) — 60 days. Play statistics in non-identifying form — up to 2 years.
· Push token (if you allow notifications) — until you turn notifications off or delete the account.
· Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking), install, launch and in-app events, device model, OS version, country, IP address — ads, analytics and ad measurement (Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook SDK) — per Google's and Meta's policies (Google Analytics: 14 months).
· Install source (Google Play Install Referrer) — counting sign-ups and purchases per ad campaign — until account deletion.

Payments are processed by Google Play and the App Store; we never receive card numbers.

2. Processors and international transfer

Supabase, Inc. (game server and database, stored in Seoul, Korea) · Cloudflare, Inc. (delivery of the web game, pictures and updates) · Google LLC and Apple Inc. (sign-in, in-app purchases, push notifications) · Google LLC (AdMob ads, Firebase analytics) · Meta Platforms, Inc. (ad measurement via the Facebook SDK) · WaveSpeedAI (making outfit drawings from your description and optional photo; the photo is deleted from our server right after). We do not sell personal data.

3. What other players can see

Your nickname, village name, village look, likes and guestbook stamps appear in other players' friend lists, rankings and telescope. Writing guestbook messages is only possible between mutual friends; you can remove friends at any time and report or block messages.

4. Your rights and account deletion

Delete your account any time in Settings › Account › Delete my village. Your village, progress, currency, friends, guestbook and wardrobe are removed permanently; only purchase records are kept for the legal period. You may also email us to request deletion, access, correction or suspension of processing.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

7. Changes

We announce changes on this page and in the Game 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes unfavorable to you).
